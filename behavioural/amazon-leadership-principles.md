# Amazon Leadership Principles

* **Customer obsession:**

  + know who are the customers
  + what their needs
  + what they really want from you
* **Ownership**

  + think long-term value over short-term results
  + act on behalf of entire company, not just own team
  + never say “that’s not my job”
  + work outside of the area
* **Invent & Simplify**

  + leaders expect and require innovation & invention from their teams
  + and always find ways to simplify
  + they look for ideas everywhere
* **Are right, A lot**

  + leaders have strong judgement and god instincts
  + leaders have amazing amount of authority to independently get things done
  + “Get forgiveness instead of permission”
  + leaders have both good judgement/instincts as well as question their own decisions and be open to counter opinions
* **Learn and be curious**

  + be open and interested in learning new things
* **Hire and develop the best**

  + help others to grow
* **Insist on the highest standards**

  + insist on the best quality delivery/product
* **Think big**

  + leaders are open to the idea of thinking bigger than the thing in front of their face
* **Bias for action**

  + speed matters in business
  + many decisions and actions are reversible and do not need extensive study
  + we value calculated risk taking
  + we believe making some decision is better than making no decision
* **Frugality**

  + accomplish more with less
  + constraints breed resourcefulness, self-sufficiency and invention
  + it’s about saving money/time/resources
  + and doing best with less resource/cost
* **Earn Trust**

  + self-critical, listen attentively, speak candidly and treat others respectfully
  + leaders should be able to recognise when they’ve made a mistake, so can correct quickly
* **Deep dive**

  + leaders are curious and skeptical
  + they know details and ask question if something doesn’t make sense
* **Have backbone; Disagree and Commit**

  + leaders respectfully challenge decisions when they disagree
  + they don’t compromise for the sake of social cohesion
* **Deliver results**

  + leaders focus on the key business inputs and deliver them with the right quality and in timely fashion
  + deliver the right results, not the perfect results
